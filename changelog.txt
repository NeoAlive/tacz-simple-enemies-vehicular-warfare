SBW: Combined Arms — Changelog
================================

0.1.2
-----
Fortifications
- Sandbag fighting positions: craftable (4 sand + 2 string), shovel-soft, directional seat pose
  from Bedrock animation; break drops sand + string (not the block).
- ENTRENCHED orders seat units on free sandbags; RU/US auto-seek sandbags alongside trenches /
  emplacements. Dismiss clears the task (not vehicle dismount); RU/US scramble on leave.
- Emplacement pad uses a custom Blockbench model/texture; pickaxe-mineable with correct tool drops.
- Trenches, foxholes, and emplacement pads remain the core of network entrenchment.

AI / command
- Soft sandbag claims stored on the block entity (no world-wide claimant scan).
- Seek cooldown after leaving a contested sandbag so infantry do not immediately re-enter.
